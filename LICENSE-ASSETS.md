## Demo music

The bundled “Field Notes” mixtape is **generated at runtime** in the browser (original synthesis, no sampled recordings). It is part of this software and covered by the MIT license.

## Fonts

Loaded from the `@fontsource` packages:

- Barlow Condensed — [OFL](https://scripts.sil.org/OFL)
- Caveat — [OFL](https://scripts.sil.org/OFL)
- Share Tech Mono — [OFL](https://scripts.sil.org/OFL)

## Mechanical sound

Key clicks, lid clunks, motor whir, and tape hiss are synthesized with the Web Audio API. No third-party samples.

## User files and recordings
